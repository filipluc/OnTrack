# OnTrack — User Guide

OnTrack is a shared calendar for school, sports, and daily routines. Each kid has their
own account to check off what they've done; parents have their own account with a full
view of everyone's schedule.

## Getting started (parent)

1. Open the app and choose **Create account** (parent sign-up).
2. Fill in your name, an email, and a password.
3. You're now signed in to your own schedule.

## Adding your child

1. From your dashboard, select **+ Add child**.
2. Enter your child's name, a login (an email address, or just something they can
   remember — it doesn't need to be a real inbox), and a password.
3. Your child can now sign in with that login on their own device, and their name
   appears as a tab next to "My schedule" on yours.

You create your child's account for them — there's no separate sign-up step they need
to go through, and no invite code to share.

## Everyday use

- **Switch schedules:** tap "My schedule" or your child's name at the top to see either
  view. Parents can see and edit both; a child only ever sees their own.
- **Change day:** use the "‹ Prev" / "Next ›" buttons to move between days.
- **Add a task:** tap **+ Add task**, fill in:
  - **Title** — what it is (e.g. "Soccer practice")
  - **Category** — School, Sport, Routine, Leisure, or Other (shows as a colored tag)
  - **Repeats** — *Just once* (pick a date), *Every day*, or *Certain days each week*
    (pick which weekdays)
  - **Start/end time** — optional
- **Mark done:** tap the checkbox next to a task. For repeating tasks, this only marks
  *that day's* occurrence — a daily task's checkbox resets fresh every morning.
- **Delete a task:** tap the ✕ next to it. This removes every future (and past)
  occurrence of that task, not just one day's — there's currently no way to skip a
  single instance of a repeating task without deleting the whole thing.

## Who can see and do what

- **Parents** can view and edit everything on their own schedule and on any of their
  children's schedules — add, edit, complete, or delete tasks either way.
- **Children** can only see and manage their own schedule. They cannot see a parent's
  tasks, another sibling's tasks, or add new child accounts.

## Tips

- Use *Routine* for the same-every-day stuff (wake up, brush teeth) with **Every day**
  recurrence, so it shows up automatically without re-adding it.
- Use *Certain days each week* for a weekly class or practice (e.g. swimming every
  Tuesday and Thursday) instead of adding it one day at a time.
- One-off events — a single game, a field trip — use **Just once** with a specific date.
